
# Applied Business Case Studies

A collection of short, instructor-reviewed applied business case studies and worked examples created for `BUS 100 — Applied Business Analysis` by Delwin Manjali. The project is a small static site showcasing analyses, slide decks, spreadsheets, and written recommendations.

Live demo: https://delwinmanjali-creator.github.io/applied-business-case-studies/

## What you'll find here

- Curated case studies with slide decks, models, and brief write-ups.
- Source artifacts (images and spreadsheets) used to illustrate analysis.

### Example case studies (selected)

- Pulse Fitness — Modeled cost, margin, and customer lifetime value across segments to recommend which market to prioritize.
	- Slides: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1EpYePJT-Ne-JCedMT4wo3jOttSbVW3GdzV95ssCsvgw/edit?usp=sharing
- Rajratan — Compared two manufacturing expansion sites on cost, risk, and market fit to recommend a new plant location.
	- Slides: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1kL-ahi9Oq61ykUQ7zGdHmQNfof31SPLzP_8S1Y2Q2BQ/edit?usp=drive_link
- Workplace Policy Case (Wk 6 Business Case) — Weighed policy consistency against employee context to resolve an HR judgment call with a formal written decision.
	- Slides: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/162ENMiXAUabjPfjVvnYsggaUwkPcVY1pzAlSBJftFgHA/edit?usp=drive_link
- Golden Careers — Diagnosed a workplace morale and turnover problem and built a data-backed recognition program to address it.
	- Slides: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/175xq04PSyeFLzIkzuJEMPyB_6herX-_61At0dvA4Szs/edit?usp=drive_link
- Forest City Tennis Club — Prepared a full general ledger, income statement, and classified balance sheet for a multi-segment business with inventory, COGS, and depreciation.
	- Spreadsheet: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1xt1c72DxxVqUEc2WbTu71kycknKAt1cpTNCROrTxhjI/edit?usp=drive_link
